Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

This application ("Email Assistant", "gws CLI") is a personal, single-user command-line tool. It is not distributed, is not published to any app store, and has exactly one user: its owner.

What it accesses

  • Gmail messages, labels and drafts — scope gmail.modify
  • Google Calendar events and calendar lists — scopes calendar.events, calendar.calendarlist.readonly

Where data is processed and stored

All processing happens locally on the owner's own computer. OAuth credentials are stored locally by the Google Workspace CLI and encrypted with a key held in the operating system keychain. Mailbox and calendar data is read directly from Google's APIs; it is not copied to any server operated by this application, because no such server exists.

Sharing

No data is shared, sold, transferred or disclosed to any third party. There is no analytics, no telemetry, and no advertising.

Retention and deletion

Message contents are read on demand and are not retained beyond the local working session. Access may be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
