Email Assistant (gws CLI)

A personal command-line assistant for one person's Gmail and Google Calendar.

This application runs entirely on its owner's own computer. There is no server, no hosted service, and no other users. It reads mail and calendar data through the Google Workspace CLI using OAuth, on behalf of its owner only.

How it works

It is a local command-line tool. The owner authenticates with their own Google account; OAuth credentials and any cached data stay on the owner's machine.
